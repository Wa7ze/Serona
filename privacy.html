Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Serona is a personal tool that posts short clips to its owner's own accounts.

What data is used

  • Basic profile information (such as account ID and display name) returned by the platform after login, used to confirm which account clips are posted to.
  • Access tokens issued by the platform, used only to upload videos to that account.

How it is stored

Data is stored only on the owner's personal computer. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics.

Deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time from the platform's app permissions settings, which invalidates the stored tokens. The owner can delete local data at any time.

Contact

Questions can be sent by opening an issue at github.com/Wa7ze/Serona/issues.
